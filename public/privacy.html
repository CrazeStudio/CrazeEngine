CrazeEngine Privacy

CrazeEngine is designed to avoid application-level search-history profiles, accounts, analytics, advertising identifiers, and third-party client scripts.

Search requests

Search queries are forwarded only to the configured search provider required to obtain results. The provider may have its own privacy policy and infrastructure logs; CrazeEngine cannot truthfully promise that an upstream provider receives no query data.

Cookies and tracking

The V1 frontend does not set application cookies, use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party JavaScript.

Limitations

Network providers and Cloudflare can still observe network metadata as part of operating the service. Privacy here means CrazeEngine does not intentionally build a user search profile.
